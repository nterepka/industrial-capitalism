Economic changes and real historical events

    Italic entries are real historical events, not results of your decisions.

    Colonial markets

    Colonies open markets, but competition over colonies increases imperial rivalry. At 100, an international crisis begins.

    Class resentment

    Wages below 35 or sustained employment below 80% increase class resentment. Better wages and employment reduce it. At 100, class warfare ends the game.
